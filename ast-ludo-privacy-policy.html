Privacy Policy

AST Ludo · Last updated: October 2026

THE SHORT VERSION

AST Ludo collects no data. The game works fully offline: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers, no third parties, and no network requests of any kind. There is nothing to opt out of — there is simply nothing collected.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Game settings, player names, and win statistics are stored only in on-device storage. They never leave your phone. Uninstalling the app permanently removes everything.

PERMISSIONS

AST Ludo requests no special permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or storage.

CHILDREN

AST Ludo is a classic family board game suitable for all ages. Since no data is collected, there are no child-privacy concerns.

CONTACT

Questions about this policy: contact.astlabs@gmail.com.

CHANGES

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will ship inside the app.
